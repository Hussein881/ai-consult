# Business Strategy: AI Support Escalation Ticket Triage Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Enterprise support and customer success teams handle hundreds or thousands of incoming tickets per day. Tier-1 teams manually read, classify, prioritize, and route each one. Tickets sit in queues longer than they should. High-severity issues get missed among low-severity noise. Engineers get pulled into issues that should have been resolved at tier-1. SLA breaches go undetected until they are already a problem.

The business cost is direct: SLA penalties, customer churn from slow responses, and engineering time wasted on issues that did not need escalation.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- VP Customer Success or VP Support
- Director of Customer Operations
- CTO or Head of Engineering (for technical triage)

Secondary influencers:
- Support team leads
- Customer success managers
- Engineering managers who receive escalations

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Support teams at B2B SaaS companies typically spend 30-40% of their triage time on tickets that could be auto-classified and routed in seconds. I build AI triage systems that cut manual routing effort by 60-70% and catch SLA risks before they breach."

Key messages:
- Reduce mean time to route (MTTR) on incoming tickets
- Catch high-severity tickets buried in queue noise
- Free tier-1 agents for genuinely complex issues
- Improve SLA compliance with automated priority detection

Discovery questions:
- How many tickets per day does your team process?
- What percentage of tickets get misrouted or escalated unnecessarily?
- What does a missed SLA cost you in penalties or customer impact?
- Do you have an existing triage process or is it ad-hoc?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Large firms propose full-platform customer service transformations at $400K-$1M. Your offer is a targeted triage assistant that improves one specific workflow in 4-8 weeks without replacing existing CRM or ticketing systems.

## Engagement and Pricing Model

Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Audit ticket categories, routing rules, SLA requirements
- Baseline triage time and misrouting rate
- Price: $8,000 - $15,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Classification and priority scoring
- Routing recommendation engine
- Draft-response generation
- Integration with Zendesk or Salesforce Service Cloud
- Price: $40,000 - $85,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- Advanced routing rules and SLA alerting
- Feedback loop for quality improvement
- Reporting dashboard
- Price: $45,000 - $100,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Category model updates as product evolves
- Price: $3,000 - $6,000/month

Typical total engagement: $85,000 - $185,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a team of 20 tier-1 agents spending 25% of time on triage at $60K/year:
- Current triage cost: 20 × $60K × 25% = $300,000/year
- Post-implementation (60% reduction): $120,000/year
- Annual savings: $180,000
- Payback period on $70K pilot: 5 months

Plus: measurable reduction in SLA breaches and escalation volume.

## Ideal First Client Profile

- B2B SaaS company with 10-100 support agents
- High ticket volume with classification complexity
- Zendesk, Salesforce, Freshdesk, or similar ticketing system
- Support leader who tracks SLA compliance and escalation rates
