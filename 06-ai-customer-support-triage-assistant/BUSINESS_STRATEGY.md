# Business Strategy: AI Customer Support Triage Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Customer support teams at B2B SaaS and e-commerce companies handle thousands of incoming tickets per week across email, chat, and web forms. Tier-1 agents manually read each ticket, decide what kind of issue it is, decide how urgent it is, decide who should handle it, and then write a response from scratch. This is slow, inconsistent, and exhausting for the agents doing it. The result is slow response times, SLA breaches, inconsistent customer experiences, and high agent turnover.

The real cost is not just agent time. It is customer churn from slow or inconsistent responses, SLA penalty payments for enterprise clients, and engineering team time wasted on escalations that should never have reached them.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- VP Customer Support or VP Customer Success
- COO / Head of Operations
- CTO (when technical triage is in scope)

Secondary influencers:
- Support team leads
- Customer success managers
- Engineering managers who receive escalation tickets

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Support teams at companies your size spend 30-40% of agent time just on classification and routing, before any real work starts. I build AI triage systems that automate that classification layer, so agents arrive at a ticket already knowing what it is, who should own it, and what the draft response looks like."

Key messages:
- Reduce first-response time
- Improve SLA compliance automatically
- Reduce misrouting and unnecessary engineering escalations
- Free agents for genuinely complex interactions

Discovery questions:
- How many support tickets per day does your team handle?
- What are the top 5 ticket categories you see most often?
- What percentage are currently misrouted or escalated unnecessarily?
- What does a missed SLA cost you in contractual penalties or churn?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Enterprise consulting firms propose CX transformation programs at $500K-$1M. Your approach is targeted: one workflow, one pilot, working results in 4-6 weeks. Clients see ROI before committing to a larger engagement.

## Engagement and Pricing Model

Phase 1: Discovery diagnostic (1-2 weeks)
- Map top ticket categories, routing rules, and current SLA performance
- Baseline manual triage time and misrouting rate
- Price: $8,000 - $15,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Classification and urgency scoring model
- Routing recommendation engine
- Draft-response generator for top categories
- Zendesk / Freshdesk / Salesforce integration
- Price: $45,000 - $90,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- SLA alerting and escalation monitoring
- Agent feedback loop for model improvement
- Reporting and performance dashboard
- Price: $40,000 - $100,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Category model updates as product evolves
- Price: $3,000 - $7,000/month

Typical total engagement: $85,000 - $195,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a team of 25 agents where 35% of time is classification and routing at $55K/year:
- Current cost: 25 × $55K × 35% = $481,000/year
- Post-implementation (65% automation): $168,000/year
- Annual savings: $313,000
- Payback period on $75K pilot: 3 months
