# Business Strategy: AI Compliance and Quality Documentation Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Companies in regulated industries spend enormous amounts of time producing, reviewing, updating, and auditing documentation. ISO 9001, ISO 27001, HIPAA, SOX, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, and similar frameworks require a constant stream of SOPs, evidence packages, gap analyses, and audit summaries. This work is largely manual, time-consuming, and often handled by expensive compliance consultants or internal staff who could be spending their time on higher-value activities.

Audit preparation is the crisis point: companies scramble for weeks before every external audit to gather evidence, update outdated docs, and fill gaps. The entire cycle is reactive instead of proactive.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- Chief Compliance Officer (CCO)
- VP Quality Assurance
- General Counsel (for SOX/legal compliance)

Secondary influencers:
- Quality managers
- Compliance managers
- Internal audit leads
- Operations directors in regulated environments

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Regulated companies I work with typically spend 3-6 weeks before every audit scrambling to update SOPs, fill documentation gaps, and produce evidence packages manually. I build AI documentation assistants that help compliance teams maintain audit-ready documentation continuously, not just before an audit."

Key messages:
- Move from reactive audit prep to continuous compliance
- Reduce time to produce SOPs and audit documentation by 70%
- Catch documentation gaps before auditors do
- Ensure consistency across sites, teams, and versions

Discovery questions:
- How many audits do you face per year, and how far in advance does prep begin?
- What is the biggest documentation challenge: creation speed, version control, or gap identification?
- Which regulatory framework matters most to your business?
- How much do compliance consultants cost you annually?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Big 4 compliance firms charge $300-$500/hour for documentation work. Custom AI documentation tools that know your specific standards, templates, and gaps reduce that external spend and free internal compliance staff for judgment-heavy work.

Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Audit current documentation state, regulatory requirements, and gap areas
- Price: $8,000 - $15,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- SOP draft generation for target framework
- Gap-analysis engine against internal standards
- Audit-readiness assessment
- Export workflow
- Price: $45,000 - $90,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- Multi-framework support
- Document version control and approval workflow
- Integration with existing quality management system
- Price: $45,000 - $100,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Updates as standards or regulations change
- Price: $3,000 - $7,000/month

Typical total engagement: $90,000 - $200,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a company spending $250K/year on external compliance consultants plus 3 months of internal staff time on audit prep:
- If AI tooling reduces external consultant use by 40%: $100,000/year savings
- If internal audit-prep time drops by 50%: additional $60,000-$100,000 in internal labor saved
- Total annual savings: $160,000-$200,000
- Payback period on $80K pilot: 5-6 months

## Ideal First Client Profile

- Documentation management done in Word/SharePoint with no automation
