# Learning Guide: AI Compliance and Quality Documentation Assistant

## What You Need to Learn

This project introduces the intersection of AI and regulated industry documentation. The key skills are understanding compliance frameworks, building document comparison and gap analysis systems, and producing outputs that meet audit and regulatory standards. This is a high-value, specialized domain.

---

## Phase 1: Compliance Frameworks Overview (Week 1)

### Core Concepts
- ISO 9001: Quality Management Systems
- ISO 27001: Information Security Management
- HIPAA: Healthcare privacy and security requirements
- SOX: Financial controls and audit requirements
- FDA 21 CFR Part 11: Electronic records in life sciences
- What auditors look for: completeness, currency, traceability

### Resources
- **YouTube**: "ISO 9001 Explained" by Mark Hammar — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0LKtfPz0vZ8
- **YouTube**: "ISO 27001 Tutorial" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tLNnPJMHX1k
- **Docs**: ISO standards overview — https://www.iso.org/standards.html
- **Article**: "SOX Compliance Guide for Beginners" — https://www.auditboard.com/blog/what-is-sox-compliance/

## Phase 2: SOP Document Structure and Templates (Week 2)

### Core Concepts

## Phase 3: LLM for Document Drafting (Week 2-3)

### Core Concepts
- Generating compliant SOP drafts from brief inputs

- Section-by-section generation with quality validation

## Phase 4: Gap Analysis and Document Comparison (Week 3-4)

### Core Concepts
- Comparing documents against a standard or template
- Identifying missing sections, outdated content, or non-conforming language
- Semantic similarity for document comparison (not just text diff)
- Generating gap reports with specific remediation recommendations

### Resources
- **Library**: `deepdiff` — https://github.com/seperman/deepdiff
- **YouTube**: "Semantic Similarity with Sentence Transformers" — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ub_PVQMGBY
- **Library**: `sentence-transformers` — https://www.sbert.net/
- **Article**: "Document Comparison with AI" — https://towardsdatascience.com/
