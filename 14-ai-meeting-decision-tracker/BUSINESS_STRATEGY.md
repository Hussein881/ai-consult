# Business Strategy: AI Meeting and Decision Tracker

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Modern knowledge workers attend 3-5 meetings per day. Each meeting produces decisions, action items, and commitments that should drive work forward. In practice, most of those decisions are never formally recorded, action items are forgotten by the following morning, and accountability is unclear. Teams re-discuss the same topics in subsequent meetings because nobody has a reliable record of what was decided.

The business cost is visible at the team and project level: missed deadlines from forgotten action items, duplicated work from unclear ownership, and time wasted in follow-up meetings that would not be needed if the first meeting had been properly captured.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- VP of Product or Engineering (for product and tech teams)
- COO (for operations-wide meetings)
- Chief of Staff (for executive meetings)

Secondary influencers:
- Project managers
- Engineering managers
- Operations leads who run recurring meetings

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Teams I work with tell me the same thing: decisions get made in meetings but nobody can reliably find them a week later, and half the action items from any given meeting are forgotten by the following morning. I build AI meeting trackers that turn meeting notes or transcripts into searchable decision logs and owner-tagged action items in minutes."

Key messages:
- Never lose a decision or action item from a meeting again
- Improve team accountability with owner-tagged task exports
- Reduce repeat-discussion meetings by making prior decisions searchable
- Produce follow-up emails automatically from meeting content

Discovery questions:
- How do you currently capture decisions and action items from meetings?
- How often do teams re-discuss decisions that should already be settled?
- What percentage of action items from meetings are completed on schedule?
- How many hours per week are spent in unnecessary follow-up meetings?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Meeting AI tools like Otter.ai and Fireflies capture transcripts but do not extract decisions, generate owner-tagged tasks, or produce a searchable decision log. Your custom implementation is built around the client's specific workflow, meeting types, and accountability framework.

- Current cost: 20 × 5 × 0.33h × $150 × 50 weeks = $247,500/year
- Plus: if one avoidable follow-up meeting per week is eliminated for each manager → additional $750,000/year in meeting capacity recovered
- Payback period on $50K pilot: immediate
