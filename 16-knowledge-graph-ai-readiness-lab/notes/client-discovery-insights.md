## Quick Instructions

1. Capture exact language the contact used when describing pain.
2. Focus on workflow cost, not just technical gaps.
3. Identify one pilot that can prove value in 4-8 weeks.
4. End with next step, owner, and due date.

## Example Entry: Recruiter Network Conversation

### Contact

- Name: Eric Wallen
- Role: President/Senior Technical Recruiter
- Industry: Technical recruiting (financial-services and technology clients)
- Relationship type: direct

### Problem Signals Mentioned

- Clients are hiring for knowledge engineering and knowledge graph roles.
- Demand includes ontology, entity modeling, data quality validation, and graph-enabled AI pipelines.

### Current Workflow Friction

- Teams struggle to connect structured and unstructured information for reliable AI use.
- Decision workflows are slowed by fragmented data and inconsistent entity definitions.

### Data and Knowledge Constraints

- Inconsistent master records across systems.
- Limited confidence in data quality for AI-backed decisions.

### AI Adoption Concerns

- Fear of low-trust outputs if knowledge foundations are weak.
- Concern that pilots fail when governance and traceability are missing.

### Potential First Pilot

- Use case: AI knowledge foundation readiness sprint + scoped graph-backed retrieval pilot.
- Users: data engineering, product/ops analysts, architecture stakeholders.
- Data sources: CRM records, policy docs, operational tickets, internal knowledge docs.
- Success metric: reduced analysis time, improved retrieval precision, lower duplicate entity rate.
- Pilot duration: 2-week readiness sprint followed by 4-8 week implementation pilot.

### Urgency and Buying Signal

- Timeline pressure: medium to high for teams currently staffing these gaps.
- Budget signal: positive where hiring reqs already exist.
- Decision owner: CTO, Head of Data, Architecture lead, Ops transformation lead.
- Procurement risk level: medium.

### Qualification Snapshot

- Problem clarity (1-5): 4
- Economic pain (1-5): 4
- Access to decision maker (1-5): 3
- Data readiness (1-5): 3
- Pilot feasibility in 8 weeks (1-5): 4

### Follow-Up Plan

- Next step: send one-page offer and ask for 2-3 warm introductions aligned to knowledge workflow pain.
- Owner: Anwar
- Due date: within 3 business days of meeting.
