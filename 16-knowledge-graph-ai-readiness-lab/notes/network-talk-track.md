# Network Talk Track

## 30-Second Version

I am building practical AI consulting offers for teams where knowledge is fragmented across systems and people are still doing too much manual synthesis. I focus on low-risk pilots that improve retrieval quality, consistency, and decision speed.

## 60-Second Version

I am building a consulting practice around practical AI deployments that depend on strong knowledge foundations. Many teams want AI outcomes but their data and knowledge model are not ready for reliable production use. I help identify one high-friction workflow, map the knowledge and entity model, add quality controls, and launch a focused pilot with measurable outcomes.

## Questions To Ask In Networking Conversations

- Where are teams spending the most manual time understanding information before making a decision?
- Which workflows break down because knowledge is spread across too many systems?
- Where is data inconsistency creating operational risk?
- Which use case would be valuable enough to justify a 4-8 week pilot?

## Closing Ask

If someone in your network is dealing with workflow bottlenecks tied to knowledge quality or retrieval reliability, I would appreciate an introduction for a short exploratory conversation.
