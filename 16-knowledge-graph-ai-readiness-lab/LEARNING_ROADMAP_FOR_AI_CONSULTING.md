# Learning Roadmap For AI Consulting

## Purpose

Build the broad, practical skill set required to help businesses realize AI advantage across multiple use cases, industries, and tool stacks.

This roadmap is intentionally general. It is not tied to one project type (for example, knowledge graphs). Instead, it helps you become effective across common consulting engagements such as:

- workflow automation
- internal knowledge assistants
- support and operations triage
- reporting and decision support
- compliance/documentation acceleration
- engineering productivity improvements

## Outcome

If you complete this roadmap, you should be able to:

1. identify business problems where AI can create measurable value
2. scope low-risk projects buyers will approve
3. deliver production-minded pilots safely
4. communicate ROI and expansion paths in executive language

## How To Use This Roadmap

- Follow the domains in sequence.
- Build at least one artifact per domain.
- Spend 60% on doing, 40% on studying.
- Start with paid discovery once you pass Gate 1; do not wait for perfection.

## Core Learning Domains

## 1) Business Discovery and Problem Framing

### Learn

- discovery interviewing
- pain quantification and cost-of-delay thinking
- process mapping (current state and bottlenecks)
- stakeholder mapping and decision process discovery

### Why It Matters

Strong problem framing is the difference between a paid engagement and an ignored AI pitch.

### Build Artifacts

- discovery questionnaire
- workflow baseline template
- meeting summary template with risks and assumptions

## 2) AI Opportunity Design

### Learn

- use-case prioritization (impact vs feasibility)
- choosing the right approach: deterministic automation, LLM workflow, predictive model, or hybrid
- human-in-the-loop design
- acceptance criteria and success metrics

### Why It Matters

Most failed projects start with the wrong use case or unclear outcome.

### Build Artifacts

- use-case scoring matrix
- pilot scope template
- decision rubric for AI vs non-AI solution choice

## 3) Data and Knowledge Readiness

### Learn

- data quality fundamentals (completeness, consistency, timeliness, accuracy)
- document and knowledge source assessment
- retrieval design basics (keyword, semantic, hybrid)
- entity consistency and governance concepts

### Why It Matters

AI quality is constrained by data and knowledge quality.

### Build Artifacts

- data readiness checklist
- source inventory sheet
- traceability and citation standard for outputs

## 4) Solution Architecture and Integration

### Learn

- API-first integration patterns
- event/batch ingestion patterns
- authentication and authorization basics
- observability and incident-handling design

### Why It Matters

Clients keep solutions that fit their environment and run reliably.

### Build Artifacts

- reference architecture diagram
- integration dependency map
- runbook for failure and fallback behavior

## 5) Delivery Engineering (Build Skills)

### Learn

- Python and service development basics
- prompt and context engineering
- evaluation harness basics (quality checks, regression tests)
- packaging and deployment fundamentals

### Why It Matters

Consultants need enough build depth to deliver outcomes, not just recommendations.

### Build Artifacts

- one working pilot prototype
- evaluation checklist
- deployment/readiness checklist

## 6) Responsible AI, Security, and Compliance

### Learn

- model and data risk controls
- privacy and data minimization principles
- access controls and audit logging
- governance practices for high-impact workflows

### Why It Matters

Trust, compliance, and risk management are often deal blockers or deal enablers.

### Build Artifacts

- AI risk register
- security and governance one-pager
- escalation policy for low-confidence outputs

## 7) Change Management and Adoption

### Learn

- user onboarding for AI workflows
- operating model changes (roles, handoffs, approvals)
- adoption metrics and feedback loops
- training and enablement plans

### Why It Matters

No adoption means no business value, even with good technical output.

### Build Artifacts

- user enablement plan
- adoption scorecard
- communication plan for stakeholders

## 8) Commercial Skills (Selling and Scoping)

### Learn

- offer design (diagnostic, pilot, hardening, retainer)
- pricing models and scope boundaries
- proposal writing and objection handling
- pipeline cadence and follow-up discipline

### Why It Matters

Technical capability without commercial execution does not create a consulting business.

### Build Artifacts

- one-page offer sheet
- SOW template
- proposal deck and follow-up sequence

## 9) Reusable IP and Firm Building

### Learn

- turning client work into reusable frameworks
- template, library, and playbook reuse patterns
- internal QA standards and delivery checklists

### Why It Matters

Reusable IP increases margin and delivery speed over time.

### Build Artifacts

- discovery kit
- pilot delivery kit
- case-study template with before/after metrics

## 90-Day General Learning Plan

## Days 1-30: Business and Scoping Foundation

Focus:

- Business Discovery and Problem Framing
- AI Opportunity Design
- Commercial Skills (core)

Outputs:

- discovery script
- opportunity scoring template
- pilot scope + acceptance criteria template

## Days 31-60: Technical Delivery Foundation

Focus:

- Data and Knowledge Readiness
- Solution Architecture and Integration
- Delivery Engineering

Outputs:

- one reference architecture
- one pilot prototype
- one evaluation and monitoring checklist

## Days 61-90: Reliability and Growth Foundation

Focus:

- Responsible AI, Security, and Compliance
- Change Management and Adoption
- Reusable IP and Firm Building

Outputs:

- risk register and governance brief
- adoption and training plan
- reusable playbook set

## Weekly Cadence

- Monday: learning and notes
- Tuesday: implementation practice
- Wednesday: artifact writing (templates, checklists)
- Thursday: client-language translation and packaging
- Friday: outreach, mock discovery, and follow-up execution

## Readiness Gates

## Gate 1: Paid Discovery Ready

You can:

- run a discovery call with clear business framing
- map current workflow pain and measurable KPIs
- propose a 1-2 week paid diagnostic

## Gate 2: Pilot Delivery Ready

You can:

- scope a 4-8 week pilot with explicit acceptance criteria
- deliver a working solution with safeguards and monitoring
- present baseline vs target metrics

## Gate 3: Expansion Ready

You can:

- show measurable pilot results
- propose phase 2 expansion opportunities
- reuse assets to reduce effort and improve margins

## Common Mistakes To Avoid

- trying to become a deep expert in every AI subdomain before selling discovery
- leading with tools/models instead of business pain and outcomes
- promising broad transformation instead of phased low-risk delivery
- skipping adoption and change management plans
- failing to convert every engagement into reusable IP

## Final Benchmark

You are positioned to thrive in AI consulting when you can consistently:

1. diagnose high-cost workflow pain in one conversation
2. design a practical AI opportunity with clear ROI logic
3. deliver a reliable pilot with governance and adoption support
4. communicate outcomes in executive language
5. scale repeatably through reusable assets and process discipline

At that stage, you are not waiting to become an expert first. You are operating like a professional while compounding expertise through delivery.
