# Exercise 01: Domain Model and Ontology Draft

## Objective

Pick one domain and create a first-pass ontology and entity map.

## Steps

1. Choose a domain: release engineering, support operations, compliance documentation, or onboarding.
2. List 15-25 key entities.
3. Define important relationships between entities.
4. Identify controlled vocabularies and status values.
5. Mark which fields are critical for decision quality.

## Deliverable

- One page with entities, attributes, and relationships.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Includes at least 15 entities.
- Includes at least 20 relationships.
- Distinguishes required vs optional attributes.
- Includes business definitions that non-engineers can understand.
