# Exercise 02: Entity Resolution and MDM Plan

## Objective

Design a practical approach for resolving duplicate or conflicting entities.

## Steps

1. Define duplicate scenarios for your domain.
2. Create matching rules (exact, fuzzy, contextual).
3. Define confidence tiers for auto-merge vs human review.
4. Specify a golden-record strategy.
5. Document data stewardship ownership.

## Deliverable

- A resolution policy with sample match logic and review workflow.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Includes at least 5 duplicate scenarios.
- Includes a confidence scoring rubric.
- Includes a human-in-the-loop checkpoint.
- Explains impact on downstream AI quality.
