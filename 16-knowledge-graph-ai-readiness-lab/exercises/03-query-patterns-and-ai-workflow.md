# Exercise 03: Query Patterns and AI Workflow Design

## Objective

Map business questions to graph queries and AI workflow steps.

## Steps

1. Write 10 business questions users ask repeatedly.
2. Define graph query logic for each question.
3. Design a retrieval pipeline: graph retrieval, ranking, response generation, citation output.
4. Define failure modes and fallback behaviors.
5. Add confidence thresholds and escalation rules.

## Deliverable

- Query-to-question matrix plus AI workflow diagram.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Contains at least 10 question-query mappings.
- Includes confidence and fallback strategy.
- Shows where human review is required.
- Includes traceability expectations for outputs.
