# Exercise 04: Quality Controls and Pilot Packaging

## Objective

Create quality controls and package your work as a buyer-ready pilot offer.

## Steps

1. Draft validation rules for key entity classes.
2. Define a data quality scorecard.
3. Document monitoring KPIs and alert conditions.
4. Build a 2-week discovery scope and 4-8 week pilot scope.
5. Write a one-page pilot proposal summary.

## Deliverable

- Quality checklist + pilot brief.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Includes validation logic for critical fields.
- Includes measurable KPIs for quality and adoption.
- Includes scope boundaries and acceptance criteria.
- Includes business outcome statement for executive readers.
