# Business Strategy: AI Invoice and Document Processing Automation

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Finance and accounts payable teams at mid-size companies process hundreds or thousands of invoices, purchase orders, and vendor documents per month by hand. Each document requires a person to open it, read it, extract field values, check them, enter them into the accounting or ERP system, and file the document. A team of 5 AP clerks processing 1,000 invoices per month typically spends 80-120 hours per month on pure data entry. Errors introduce payment delays, audit findings, and duplicate payments.

The deeper problem is that this scales linearly with business growth. Every new vendor relationship and every new revenue dollar adds more document volume. Companies solve this by hiring more AP staff, which is expensive and still error-prone.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- CFO / VP Finance
- Controller or Accounting Manager
- VP Operations

Secondary influencers:
- AP Manager
- IT Director (for ERP integration)
- Finance operations leads

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Finance teams at companies your size typically spend 80-120 hours per month on manual invoice data entry that could be fully automated. I build AI document processing systems that extract, validate, and export invoice data ready for your accounting system, cutting that work by 85%."

Key messages:
- Eliminate manual data entry from AP workflow
- Reduce duplicate payments and keying errors
- Improve vendor payment cycle time
- Scale AP processing without adding headcount

Discovery questions:
- How many invoices per month does your AP team process?
- What is the current manual processing time per document?
- How many errors per month result in payment delays or duplicates?
- What accounting or ERP system are you currently using?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Enterprise document automation platforms (ABBYY, Kofax, SAP) require 6-12 month implementations at $200K-$500K. Your pilot deploys targeted AI extraction for the specific document types and ERP your client uses in 4-6 weeks with clear accuracy benchmarks.

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Ingestion for invoices and top document types

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)

- Approval workflow

Phase 4: Retainer

Typical total engagement: $75,000 - $165,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For an AP team of 4 clerks spending 50% of time on data entry at $55K/year:
- Current cost: 4 × $55K × 50% = $110,000/year
- Post-implementation (85% automation): $16,500/year
- Annual savings: $93,500
- Plus: reduction in payment errors, duplicates, and audit findings adds additional value
- Payback period on $60K pilot: 8 months
