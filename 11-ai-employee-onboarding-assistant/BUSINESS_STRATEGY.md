# Business Strategy: AI Employee Onboarding Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

High-growth companies hire in waves. Each wave of new hires creates the same bottleneck: managers and senior team members get bombarded with the same questions, HR is overwhelmed with basic onboarding requests, and new employees feel unsupported and take longer to become productive. The cost compounds at scale.

The harder problem is consistency. In companies with multiple offices, remote teams, or high turnover, onboarding quality varies drastically. What one new hire learns in week one, another learns in week four by accident. This inconsistency affects both performance and retention.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- Chief People Officer (CPO) or VP of HR
- COO (when operations-wide)
- VP Talent or Director of Employee Experience

Secondary influencers:
- HR business partners
- People operations managers
- Department heads with high hiring rates

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Every company in a hiring phase runs into the same problem: managers and HR spend 5-10 hours per new hire answering the same questions, and new hires still feel unsupported in week one. I build AI onboarding assistants that answer those questions automatically from your own onboarding materials, cutting manager overhead by 50% and improving new-hire confidence."

Key messages:
- Cut onboarding ramp time by 20-40%
- Free managers from repetitive onboarding questions
- Give every new hire consistent, accurate information
- Track onboarding gaps with unanswered-question analytics

Discovery questions:
- How long does it take a new hire to become fully productive?
- What are the most common questions new hires ask in the first 30 days?
- How much time do managers spend on onboarding support per new hire?
- How consistent is the onboarding experience across teams or offices?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

HRIS and LMS vendors propose platform implementations at $150K-$400K annually. Your offer is a targeted AI Q&A assistant that works with your existing documents and deploys in 4-6 weeks, with clear ramp-time and satisfaction metrics.

## Engagement and Pricing Model

Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Audit onboarding materials, common questions, and role-specific gaps
- Price: $7,000 - $12,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Ingestion of onboarding docs and role guides
- Q&A assistant with source-grounded answers
- Role-based onboarding paths
- Checklist and progress tracking
- Price: $35,000 - $70,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- Additional role coverage and department expansion
- Manager dashboard and analytics
- Slack or Teams integration
- Price: $30,000 - $70,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Content updates as company grows
- Price: $2,000 - $4,000/month

Typical total engagement: $65,000 - $145,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a 100-person company hiring 30 new employees per year, with managers spending 8 hours/new hire on onboarding support at $150/hour:
- Current cost: 30 × 8 × $150 = $36,000/year in manager time (just for onboarding Q&A)
- Plus: if productivity ramp shortens from 10 weeks to 7 weeks at $3,000/week value → $90,000/year in earlier productivity
- Total value: $126,000/year
- Payback period on $60K pilot: 6 months

## Ideal First Client Profile

- HR team overwhelmed by onboarding overhead
- CPO or HR leader who tracks ramp time and early attrition
