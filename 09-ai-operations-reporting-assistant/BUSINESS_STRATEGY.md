# Business Strategy: AI Operations Reporting Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Engineering managers, department heads, and operations teams manually compile status reports every week. They pull data from Jira, spreadsheets, dashboards, CRMs, and team messages, write summaries by hand, and format them for leadership. A single weekly report can take 2-4 hours. For a company with 20 managers each spending 2 hours per week on reporting, that is 2,000 hours per year of leadership capacity consumed by formatting text.

The output is also often poor: inconsistent formats, missing blockers, buried risks, and summaries that do not actually help executives make decisions.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- COO / VP Operations
- VP Engineering (for engineering org reporting)
- Chief of Staff (for executive reporting cadence)

Secondary influencers:
- Department heads and senior managers
- Business analysts
- Operations managers

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Most management teams I work with have the same problem: their weekly reporting cycle consumes 2-4 hours of manager time and produces summaries that are inconsistent and hard to act on. I build AI reporting systems that generate structured weekly operating reports automatically, so managers spend 15 minutes reviewing instead of 3 hours assembling."

Key messages:
- Cut weekly reporting time by 80%
- Improve executive visibility into blockers and risks
- Standardize reporting format across teams
- Surface risks and blockers before they become incidents

Discovery questions:
- How long does your management team spend assembling weekly reports?
- How many people are involved in producing or reviewing weekly status updates?
- Do executives feel they have accurate, timely visibility into operational risks?
- What are the data sources your reporting currently pulls from?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Analytics and BI firms pitch full operational dashboards at $200K-$500K. Your offer is a targeted AI reporting layer that works with existing tools and data sources, producing narrative summaries executives can act on in 4-6 weeks.

## Engagement and Pricing Model

Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Map reporting workflow, data sources, and executive review format
- Identify top blockers and risk categories
- Price: $7,000 - $12,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Operational input collection workflow
- AI-generated weekly summary
- Blocker and risk identification
- Executive digest format
- Price: $35,000 - $75,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- Multi-source integration (Jira, spreadsheets, CRM)
- Distribution workflow (email, Slack, PDF)
- Historical reporting and trend analysis
- Price: $35,000 - $80,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Template updates and format adjustments
- Price: $2,500 - $5,000/month

Typical total engagement: $70,000 - $160,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a company with 20 managers spending 2.5 hours/week on reporting at $125/hour:
- Current cost: 20 × 2.5 × $125 × 50 weeks = $312,500/year
- Post-implementation (80% reduction): $62,500/year
- Annual savings: $250,000
- Payback period on $60K pilot: 3 months
