# Business Strategy: AI Sales Proposal and Quote Assistant

## The Business Problem You Are Solving

Sales teams at services companies and B2B SaaS firms spend 3-8 hours per proposal writing statements of work, pricing summaries, and solution descriptions manually. That means a sales rep closing 4 deals per month loses 12-32 hours per month to proposal writing rather than selling. Proposal quality is also inconsistent: junior reps produce weaker proposals than senior reps, and best practices from winning proposals rarely get reused systematically.

The deeper problem is pipeline velocity. If proposals take longer to produce, deals close later. If proposals are weaker, close rates drop. Both outcomes directly reduce revenue.

## Target Buyers and Titles

Primary decision makers:
- Chief Revenue Officer (CRO) or VP Sales
- VP of Sales Operations
- CEO of mid-size services firm (for owner-operator businesses)

Secondary influencers:
- Sales operations leads
- Senior account executives
- Presales and solution engineering teams

## How To Pitch This

Opening line:

"Sales teams producing custom proposals manually lose 3-8 hours per proposal to writing work that could be automated. I build AI proposal assistants that cut production time by 70%, improve consistency, and help every rep produce proposals at the quality level of your best closers."

Key messages:
- Reduce proposal cycle time
- Improve consistency across the sales team
- Capture and reuse best practices from winning proposals
- Let sales reps spend time selling, not writing

Discovery questions:
- How long does it take your team to produce a proposal or SOW today?
- How many proposals does the team produce per week or month?
- How consistent is proposal quality across the team?
- What is your current proposal win rate, and do you know why you lose?

## Why They Choose You Over a Large Firm

Sales enablement platforms (Seismic, Showpad) cost $50K-$200K/year in licensing and require long configuration. Custom AI proposal tools built to your specific products, pricing, and win patterns outperform generic platforms for complex B2B services firms.

## Engagement and Pricing Model

Phase 1: Discovery (1-2 weeks)
- Collect winning proposal examples, pricing structures, SOW templates
- Define input fields and proposal components
- Price: $8,000 - $12,000

Phase 2: Pilot build (4-6 weeks)
- Proposal template and example library
- AI draft generation from client input
- SOW, scope, and pricing section generation
- PDF/DOCX export
- Price: $40,000 - $80,000

Phase 3: Hardening (4-8 weeks)
- CRM integration (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Proposal version tracking
- Win/loss tagging and feedback loop
- Price: $40,000 - $90,000

Phase 4: Retainer
- Template updates and model tuning as offers evolve
- Price: $2,500 - $5,000/month

Typical total engagement: $80,000 - $175,000

## ROI Story To Tell

For a team of 15 reps producing 3 proposals each per month, spending 5 hours each at $100 fully-loaded:
- Current cost: 15 × 3 × 5 × $100 × 12 = $270,000/year
- Post-implementation (70% reduction): $81,000/year
- Annual savings: $189,000
- Plus: if pipeline velocity improves by 10%, revenue impact dwarfs the implementation cost

## Ideal First Client Profile

- B2B services or SaaS company with a structured sales team
- Proposals and SOWs are custom per client, not catalog pricing
- 10-100 sales reps or account executives
- Sales VP who tracks proposal quality and win rate
