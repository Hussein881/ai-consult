§ 02 — Services

We organise our work into seven practices. Most engagements draw from two or three — the world rarely arrives in a single category.

A · 01

Intelligent Agents.

Multi-step, tool-using agents that handle real workflows — research, operations, internal ops, customer-facing tasks. We design for reliability before cleverness: tight scopes, deterministic guardrails, human handoff when the agent isn't sure.

Best forRepeated, multi-step knowledge work.
Typical outcome5–20× throughput on a defined task.
Engagement6–10 weeks.
A · 02

Retrieval & Knowledge.

RAG done properly. Hybrid retrieval, reranking, citation, freshness, access control. We build knowledge systems that survive contact with real users and grow gracefully as your corpus does.

Best forLarge, evolving document collections.
Typical outcomeMinutes-to-seconds on answer lookup.
Engagement4–8 weeks.
A · 03

Conversational Interfaces.

Voice and chat assistants that handle the messy middle of real conversations — interruptions, context switches, ambiguity. We design conversation flows the way good UX teams design forms: deliberately, and with the user in the room.

Best forCustomer service, intake, support.
Typical outcome30–60% deflection with high CSAT.
Engagement8–12 weeks.
A · 04

Document Intelligence.

Extract structured signal from unstructured documents — contracts, invoices, claims, research papers. We combine classical NLP, layout-aware models and LLMs, because no single approach handles real-world documents alone.

Best forHigh-volume document workflows.
Typical outcome90%+ extraction with audit trail.
Engagement6–10 weeks.
A · 05

Vision Systems.

Image and video understanding for inspection, retail, security, accessibility. We've shipped on edge devices and in the cloud, and we know which problems each is suited for.

Best forQuality, search, moderation, safety.
Typical outcomeSub-100ms inference at edge.
Engagement8–14 weeks.
A · 06

Decision Support.

Forecasts, recommendations, risk scores — built the way analysts trust. Calibrated probabilities, honest confidence intervals, and explanations the business can actually read.

Best forPricing, risk, planning, prioritisation.
Typical outcome2–8% lift on the metric that matters.
Engagement6–12 weeks.
A · 07

AI Infrastructure.

The unglamorous half of any real AI system — evaluation pipelines, observability, guardrails, prompt and model version control, cost tracking, red-teaming. We retrofit this onto existing systems and build it into new ones.

Best forTeams shipping AI in production.
Typical outcomeFrom "vibes" to measurable systems.
Engagement4–8 weeks.
