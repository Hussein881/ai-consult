Most engagements draw from two or three practice areas — the world rarely arrives in a single category. Below is a working catalog of what we do, how we engage, and what to expect.

Intelligent agents.

Multi-step, tool-using agents that handle real workflows — research, operations, internal ops, customer-facing tasks. We design for reliability before cleverness: tight scopes, deterministic guardrails, human handoff when the agent isn't sure.
